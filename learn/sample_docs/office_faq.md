# Halcyon Robotics — Lisbon Office FAQ

## When is the office open?

The Lisbon office is open from 07:00 to 21:00 on working days. Access outside these hours requires approval from the facilities team.

## How do visitors get in?

Visitors must be registered in the visitor system at least 24 hours in advance. They collect a temporary badge at reception and must be accompanied by their host at all times. Visitors can use the "halcyon-guest" Wi-Fi network; the password changes every Monday and is printed on the badge.

## I lost my badge. What now?

Report it at reception. A replacement badge costs €20 and is deducted from your next expense report. The lost badge is deactivated immediately.

## Is there food?

The cafeteria serves breakfast from 08:00 to 10:00 and lunch from 12:00 to 14:30. Lunch is subsidised; employees pay €4.50 per meal. Fruit and coffee are free all day.

## Where can I park?

There is no car parking for employees. Bike parking is in the basement on level -1, with showers and lockers next to it. The office is a five-minute walk from the metro.

## Can I book a meeting room?

Meeting rooms are booked through the calendar. Rooms on the third floor are reserved for customer meetings on Thursdays. The robot test hall on the ground floor can only be booked by the hardware teams.

## Who do I contact?

Facilities: facilities@halcyon.example. IT help desk: extension 4000. Security incidents: security@halcyon.example.
