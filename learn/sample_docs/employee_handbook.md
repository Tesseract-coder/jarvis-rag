# Halcyon Robotics — Employee Handbook

Halcyon Robotics is a fictional company used for the JARVIS learning exercises. Our headquarters is in Lisbon, with a smaller engineering hub in Porto.

## Working hours and remote work

Halcyon is a hybrid company. Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week; the remaining days are spent in the office so that teams can collaborate in person. Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 Lisbon time, and every meeting that involves more than one team should be scheduled inside core hours. Outside core hours you are free to arrange your day as you see fit, as long as your manager knows how to reach you.

Fully remote arrangements are possible for roles that have been approved as remote by the People team. Such approvals are reviewed once a year.

## Annual leave

Every full-time employee receives 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, in addition to Portuguese public holidays. Part-time employees receive leave pro rata.

Leave should be requested in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance for absences longer than three days. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the next year. Carried-over days expire on March 31 of the following year and cannot be paid out.

## Sick leave

Employees have 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A doctor's note is required after 3 consecutive days of absence. Please inform your manager before 10:00 on the first day you are unwell.

## Parental leave

The primary caregiver receives 20 weeks of fully paid parental leave. The secondary caregiver receives 6 weeks of fully paid leave, which can be taken at any time during the child's first year.

## Probation and notice

New employees have a probation period of 6 months. After probation, the notice period is 2 months for both the employee and the company.

## Learning and development

Each employee has a learning budget of €1,500 per year for courses, books and conferences. Unused budget does not roll over. Conference travel is booked through the travel desk and does not count against the learning budget.

## Equipment

New starters choose between a 14-inch or 16-inch laptop on their first day. Monitors, keyboards and headsets can be ordered through the IT portal. Equipment remains company property and must be returned within 10 working days of leaving.
